Stats 101

I don't record errors or sacrifices because the stat book I bought from walmart does not have a field for that. Because of that some of my definitions vary slightly from official MLB definitions.

A cool website to checkout if you are interested in reading more about baseball stats is Baseball Almanac.

Definitions

At Bat (AB) - recorded when a batter reaches a base or is put out. Walks (BB) do not count as at-bats.

BB - "Base on Balls", better known as a walk. Occurs when a pitcher throws enough balls that the batter is awarded first base. This is a fun stat because it is a measure of both a pitcher's skill and a batter's eye.

Run - recorded when a player crosses home plate to score their team a run

RBI - Runs Batted In. A batter is awarded an RBI when a run is scored as a result of their plate appearance.

Hit - when a batter strikes the baseball and reaches base. There are 4 types of hits: singles (1B), doubles (2B), triples (3B), and home runs (HR).

Batting Average - Hits / At-Bats - most common measure of a hitter's success at the plate.

On Base % - (Hits + BB) / (At-Bats + BB) - measure of how frequently a batter reaches base per plate appearance.

Slugging % - (1B + 2*2B + 3*3B + 4*HR)/AB - represents the total number of bases recorded per at-bat. Nice for identifying big beefy boys that can hit balls to the moon.

Putout - credited to the fielder who physically records the act of completing an out. Firstbasemen generally have the highest putout totals.

Assist - awarded to a fielder who touches the ball before a putout is recorded by another fielder.

Range Factor - (Putouts + Assists) / Games Played - defensive metric to evaluate how many plays a defender is involved with per game.

ERA - Earned Run Average - 9 * Runs / Innings Pitched - stat to evaluate how many runs a pitcher would allow in a 9 inning game. This is the most common stat for evaluating pitchers, but it is the stat most impacted by my inability to measure errors. As a result, pitchers in games I record are held responsible for every run, where in the big leagues errors by the defense are factored into this stat (sorry).

WHIP - (Walks + Hits) / Innings Pitched - evaluates how well a pitcher can keep people off base.
