Mexican Dinner Party

These are the recipes for a mexican dinner party I had with my friends on 08/30/2026. Each block is a course of the meal, all recipes on this page are vegetarian and gluten free. The only substitutions that need to be made for the gluten free recipes are gluten free soy sauce and 1-1 gluten free flour in the fritters. This party was for 8 people.

Spiced Espresso Martini

Ingredients

  • 1 750 mL bottle of vodka
  • 3 ½ tablespoons instant cafe de olla powder
  • 9 oz khalula
  • 1 oz water
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 1 star anise

Method

  1. Pour off 11 oz of vodka and use for something else
  2. Directly into the liquor bottle, add all the remaining ingredients
  3. Let sit in freezer for at least 1 day
