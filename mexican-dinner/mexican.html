Mexican Dinner Party

These are the recipes for a mexican dinner party I had with my friends on 08/30/2026. Each block is a course of the meal, all recipes on this page are vegetarian and can be made gluten free. The only substitutions that need to be made for the gluten free recipes are gluten free soy sauce and 1-1 gluten free flour in the fritters. This party was for 8 people.

Spiced Espresso Martini

Ingredients

  • 1 750 mL bottle of vodka
  • 3 ½ tablespoons instant cafe de olla powder
  • 9 oz khalula
  • 1 oz water
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 1 star anise

Method

  1. Pour off 11 oz of vodka and use for something else
  2. Directly into the liquor bottle, add all the remaining ingredients
  3. Let sit in freezer for at least 1 day

Banchan

Rice

Ingredients

  • 2 c water
  • 2 T butter
  • 1 t salt
  • 2 c rice, washed

Method

  1. Bring all ingredients except for rice to a light boil
  2. Add rice and stir
  3. Bring back to a boil. As soon as boil starts, reduce to simmer, cover for 16 minutes
  4. Remove from heat and let sit 10 minutes, covered

Sweet Pickled Red Onions

Ingredients

  • 1 red onion, halved and sliced through root end
  • 1 c red wine vinegar
  • 1/3 c sugar
  • 1/4 t salt

Method

  1. Bring vinegar, salt, adn sugar to a simmer until sugar is dissolved
  2. Pour vinegar over onion, cover and let cool (~1h)

Braised Tofu

Ingredients

  • 1 Block extra firm tofu, pressed for 10 min
  • 1/2 white onion, chopped into chunks
  • 1 medium roma tomato, cut into chunks
  • 1 T tomato paste
  • 2 clove garlic
  • 1 t red wine vinegar
  • 2 chipoltes from can of chiles in adobo sauce
  • 2 T adobo sauce
  • 1/4 - 1/2 c water
  • Salt, black pepper, chili powder, cumin, oregano, coriander
  • Green onion, chopped - garnish

Method

  1. Combine all sauce ingredients in a food processor
  2. Cook tofu in a lil bit of oil until browned on both sides
  3. Add sauce to pan with tofu, simmer until sauce is the consistency you want

Spicy Pickled Carrots

Ingredients

  • 1 lb carrots, peeled, sliced to even sizes
  • Olive oil
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1/4 white onion, in half moons
  • 1 can La Morena sliced jalepenos, including brine
  • 1 c white vinegar
  • 1/4 t oregano
  • 1/4 t thyme
  • Salt

Method

  1. Saute carros in olive oil with bay lead - want carrots to brighten not get soft
  2. Add onion and cook until translucent
  3. Add all other not-fresh ingredients and bring to a gentle boil
  4. Add any remaining fresh herbs and simmer for 1 minute
  5. Remove from heat and transfer to jar
  6. Ideally, marinate for at least 24h

Salsa Martajada

Ingredients

  • 1 red onion
  • 3 jalepeno
  • 3 roma tomato
  • 6 clove garlic
  • Red wine vinegar
  • chopped cilantro
  • 1 diced avocado

Method

  1. Char pepper, tomatoes, and garlic in a neutral oil, you don't want them to cook all the way through
  2. Chop in food processor with salt until chunky
  3. balance acidity with red wine vinegar
  4. add cilantro and avocado chunks just before serving

Salsa Morita

Ingredients

  • 4 dried Morita
  • 2 dried chile de arbol
  • 2 dried guajillo
  • 2 serranos
  • 2 roma tomato
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 clove garlic
  • white vinegar

Method

  1. Cut veg into large chunks and deseed dry chiles
  2. Broil fresh veg with neutral oil and salt until they take on color, 10-15 min
  3. Cook dry chiles on low heat in a lil oil until they start to take on heat. These burn so fast
  4. Blend everything, including the chili oil you just made, with vinegar, water, and salt
  5. Simmer salsa on stove for 15 minutes

Nopales

Ingredients

  • 3 medium nopales, clean and prep, diced
  • salt & pepper
  • juice of 2 limes

Method

  1. Bring a small pot of water to a boil, add nopales and salt. Simmer until all the slimy liquid is released (10-15 min)
  2. Drain and rinse nopales with fresh water to remove slimy liquid
  3. season with lime juice, salt, and pepper

Cafe de Olla Flan

8 6oz ramekins

Ingredients

  • 3/4 c granulated sugar
  • 4 c half and half
  • 1/2 c cafe de olla instant coffee powder
  • 2 t vanilla extract
  • pinch of salt
  • 6 egg yolks, room temp
  • 2 eggs, room temp
  • 50 grams chopped pilonchillo + 50g granulated sugar
  • cinnamon stick
  • star anise
  • clove

Method

  1. In a small saucepan, combine the ¾ c granulated sugar and ¼ c water and stir until the mixture is a deep amber color
  2. Quickly pour the caramel into the ramekins. Before the caramel sets, tilt the ramekins to fill any gaps
  3. Preheat oven to 300F
  4. In a small saucepan, combine half and half, spices, coffee, and salt. Heat until mixture is steaming and just starting to ripple beneath the surface, then remove from heat and set aside
  5. In a bowl, whisk all eggs, the remaining sugar, and the vanilla until well combined
  6. While constantly stirring the egg mixture, slowly pour in the hot half and half to temper, then scrape the bottom and sides with a spatula to make sure all sugar is dissolved
  7. Pass custard through a fine mesh sieve, then divide evenly among the ramekins
  8. Put ramekins in a high walled pan, pour water fresh off a boil into the pan, almost all the way to the top of the ramekins
  9. Bake for about 25 minutes until set - want a wobble not a jiggle (whole thing moves as 1, don't want any ripples)
