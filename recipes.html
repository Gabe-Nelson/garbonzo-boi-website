Buffalo Dip

Ingredients

  • 2 can chickpeas, mash half of them with fork
  • 8oz cream cheese
  • 1 c. ranch
  • 2/3 c. Frank's Red hot
  • garlic & onion powder, salt, and pepper
  • 2 c. shredded cheese; half mixed in, half for garnish
  • 1/2 c. green onion; garnish

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 350F
  2. Mix all non garnish items together
  3. Bake until bubbly, 20-25 min

Slow Cooker Chili

Ingredients

  • 1/2 red onion, diced
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 2 carronts, diced
  • 2 celery, diced
  • 28 oz diced tomatos with juice
  • 2 can blank beans, rinsed
  • 1 can pinto beans, rinsed
  • 2 chipoltes, minced + 2 T adobo sauce
  • 1 can corn, drained
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • chili powder, cumin, paprika, salt, pepper
  • adjust to desired consistency with veggie stock, ~2 c.
  • lime juice, finish - after cooking

Method

  1. Cook in slow cooker on high for 4-5h or on low for 6-7h
